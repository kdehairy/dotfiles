4. Create a logical sequence of steps that leads to successful implementation.

When creating plans, be:
- Detailed and comprehensive
- Logical and well-structured
- Realistic in terms of implementation feasibility
- Flexible enough to accommodate variations
- Explicit about dependencies and requirements
